# Whereas — comprehensive UI review, current + planned (2026-07-05 evening)

**Cowork review from a live walk of every prod surface (build ea740d5) plus the planned conversational state (mockup + C1-CONV-DESIGN v1.1). Complements, not replaces, `UI_ATTORNEY_SWEEP_1_audit_and_dispatch_2026-07-05.md` — that brief's G1–G5 tone/altitude patterns stand; this review adds the visual-identity, information-architecture, and interaction findings from tonight's sweep. Items marked ➜S# were folded into the sweep dispatch addendum.**

## Verdict in one paragraph

The bones are right: the warm-paper/oxblood identity is distinctive and appropriate, the matter page's status-strip altitude is correct, and Supervision/Diagnostics are already at the right register. The three things holding the UI back: (1) **two design languages coexist** — roughly half the surfaces got the Whereas identity and half are still generic-Tailwind (blue chips, gray text, black toggles), and the page that matters most (DocumentDetail, the drafting core) is the least converted; (2) **status is displayed but not navigable** — chips and counts tell you things but almost nothing is clickable, so every fact costs a hunt; (3) **the working surfaces are drowned by low-frequency surfaces** — nav order, Overview noise, and dead-end Templates all put machinery in front of work. All three are display-lane fixes.

## Global findings (new; G6–G9 in the dispatch addendum)

**G6 — Finish the visual identity, one accent system.** Off-brand elements found tonight: blue "Intake" phase chips (Matters list, matter header); black/dark Settings toggles (should be oxblood-on); washed-pink "Format Document" button that reads neither enabled nor disabled; Word-default-blue links in DocumentDetail; generic gray body text on DocumentDetail vs warm ink elsewhere. Rule: every interactive accent is the oxblood ramp; every status chip uses the ok/warn/neutral ramp from the deed status-strip work; no raw Tailwind blue/gray-400 anywhere.

**G7 — Everything that states a fact is a link to that fact.** The matter status strip ("Conflicts · Not yet run", "Client / parties · Walter Test…") is text; Diagnostics' "8 possibly-stuck session(s)" looks like a link and isn't; Overview's "0 open" badges don't open the matter. Pattern: chip click → the surface that manages it (parties chip → parties panel; document chip → document; stuck-sessions → the session list). This single pattern fixes most "how do I get to X" confusion, including how Kelly couldn't find party management.

**G8 — Consistent app shell.** DocumentDetail renders with no sidebar (full-bleed with breadcrumbs) while every other page keeps the rail — jarring mode switch and you lose global nav exactly where you spend the most time. Either keep the rail (collapsed to icons, RELAYOUT-3 already supports this) or make the full-bleed intentional and consistent for all document work. Also: build-stamp footer shows different values on different pages (ea740d5 vs cf1e3e2) — pick one source; and the sidebar "kelly" clips under the wordmark at some widths.

**G9 — Silent mutation failures are a class, not incidents.** Found three tonight: template Activate (412, nothing shown), document extractVariables (412, nothing shown), Overview add-deliverable (error never rendered). Rule: every useGuardedMutation call site renders `error` — a one-line toast/inline message. Cheap, global, and it would have made FL-17 obvious months ago.

## Per-surface notes (current state)

- **Matters list.** 20 of 22 rows are identical "Quick Deed — <date> · Intake" — the list gives no matter identity. Add client name + document count + last-activity to the row (data already exists), and **multi-select archive/delete** so synthetic-matter cleanup isn't 20 one-by-one dialogs. The "Next 30 days" deadline card is good; its "You are responsible for monitoring these" line is already slated for deletion (S5).
- **New Matter / New Document modals.** Fine. One improvement: after Create Matter, navigate into the matter instead of back to the list — the next action is always inside it. The New Document dialog's auto-bound "Testator: Walter Testvendor (sole client)" line is exactly the right pattern (state a resolution, don't ask).
- **Matter detail.** Status strip = right idea, make chips clickable (G7). The collapsible panels are labeled in build jargon — "Matter Intake & Analysis (Layer 0)" means nothing to an attorney; rename plainly ("Intake analysis", "Practice knowledge", "Matter state"). Engagement Capacity card takes the most prominent slot on every matter — after election it should collapse to its chip (the green "Elected" chip already exists). Parties have no visible management surface at all → interim **Add client** button in the header now (per the 7/5 ruling), full parties card arrives with the conversational build. Header buttons: FL-18.
- **DocumentDetail.** The most-used and least-converted page: legacy typography, no shell, no identity, and the template-mode controls dead-end (FL-17). Needs the identity pass most — document content in Fraunces on a paper "page" (the deed-page layout dispatch is the template), chrome in Inter, tabs styled like the rest of the app.
- **Overview.** FL-16 (affordance, sorting, silent errors) — plus once Matters rows carry deliverable counts (above), consider whether Overview earns its nav slot at all or becomes a Matters-list lens ("sort by open deliverables"). Flagged as a question, not a directive.
- **Deed.** Intake-first collapse is right. Yellow lecture → S2 (already dispatched). "Propose the facts" button wraps to two lines (same fix as FL-18). Otherwise the strongest page in the app.
- **Templates.** Dead end (FL-17). Until the pipeline is wired: badge the page "template drafting not yet wired" or hide the Activate affordance — an attorney should never meet a button that cannot ever succeed.
- **Upload & Format.** Duplicate format notes → S8. Button color (G6). Otherwise fine.
- **Settings.** (a) "Force Show All" + "Force Hide All" mutually-exclusive toggle pair → one segmented control (Auto / Always show / Always hide); (b) Gemini toggle shows enabled while the reviewer panel policy holds Gemini dormant — either reflect the dormancy pin or caption it ("enabled in config; dormant pending calibration") so Settings never contradicts policy; (c) section order after FL-19 lands: Reviewers → Conflicts & gates → Notifications → Voice/input; (d) the mid-page blank-region render glitch on scroll reproduced tonight (FL-11 class). Toggles to oxblood (G6).
- **Supervision.** Right altitude. Two touches: provider filter is a free-text "e.g. anthropic" box → make it a select (there are four providers); model ids truncate ("anthropic:…") where there's room to show them. Coverage gap (reviewer/deed egress not shown) remains FL-1, non-display.
- **Diagnostics.** Fine. Make "8 possibly-stuck session(s)" either a real link or plain text (G7); the stale failed-job counts are FL-12 (reaper), not display.
- **Notifications.** Bell + dropdown + mark-all is right; positioning at nav-bottom is fixed by FL-15.

## Planned state (mockup + C.4–C.6) — what to watch

The mockup (`CONVERSATIONAL_MATTER_UI_MOCKUP_2026-07-05.html`, with the 7/5 rulings) resolves the biggest IA problem — the matter page becomes one conversation with state at the side, and parties/facts/documents stop being buried panels. Watch items for the build: **one composer substrate** (NC-C1-4 — the matter conversation, copilot, and drafting-rail composer must be the same component with the same egress path, or we'll ship three chat boxes that behave differently); **the rail is route-only** (NC-C1-3 — no edit affordances creep into rail cards); **drop-target feedback** (drag-drop→Materials needs a visible full-page drop state so drops feel received); and the sweep's G1–G5 apply to every new surface at build time (S10 carry-forward). Interim wins that shouldn't wait for C.4–C.6: Add client button, clickable strip chips, plain-language panel labels.

## Suggested order (evidence-strength ordering, not a roadmap)

Nav + noise first (FL-15/16/19 + Matters-list identity) because they touch every session; then DocumentDetail identity pass (highest-traffic page, worst state) alongside the two deed dispatches; then the G6/G7 global pass; Settings/Supervision touches ride along. FL-17 (template pipeline) is a functional build, not display — sequence it against your appetite for template-mode drafting, since iterative mode carries all current work.

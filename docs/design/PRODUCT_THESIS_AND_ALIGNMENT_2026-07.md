## (a) The product thesis — one page

**Whereas is fundamentally a matter-state-and-judgment system — a workbench where an attorney reviews artifacts, disposes of model and reviewer output, and holds verified state across a matter's life — with drafting attached. It is not a drafting tool with review bolted on.**

The evidence forces this. Generation from a blank page is the single largest genre (~33% of threads) but a **minority** of the work. Roughly 40–45% of threads *open with an artifact to react to* — a title abstract, an executed PSA, a 17-file client folder, a counterparty redline, the attorney's own paste-in research — not with an instruction to draft. And **every multi-round thread in the corpus contains at least one operator correction of the model.** The disposition loop is the only truly universal surface. The most-loaded object is not a document; it is the *verified state of the matter* — the settled terms, confirmed facts, locked decisions, and open items that Kelly today re-assembles by hand every time a thread hits the context wall (ERGUN: "the other thread got too big, and I couldn't generate a context note").

So the product's center of gravity is **judgment work on artifacts and model output, persisted as durable matter-state, organized by the attorney's capacity (title agent / law-firm counsel / outside GC)** — the exact axis Kelly's own three projects already use. Drafting is a first-class attached capability, not the identity.

**What Whereas must do exceptionally (the top archetypes, by evidence):**
1. **The disposition/correction loop** (move 1, universal) — adopt/modify/reject/hold/pass, per-point and batch, every act audited. This is the most-used surface in the corpus; it should be the most optimized surface in the product.
2. **Persist verified matter-state that survives context limits** — the thing Kelly hand-rolls today. This is the product's reason to exist, ranked above drafting.
3. **Review artifacts and sets, spotting issues before drafting** (A2, ~21%) — including cross-document/suite consistency and blocker triage on uploaded or connector-supplied families.
4. **Multi-model review + synthesis with input-parity** (move 5, CORE) — panel fan-out, verify reviewer claims, arbitrate on a source-completeness manifest (row 38).

**What it must do excellently but not centrally:** instrument drafting from intake (A1) — per-instrument excellence is non-negotiable (the Stage-2 parity gate is literally a no-more-rework drafting test), but drafting is not the identity and the build is not organized around it; correspondence generation (A4) — high-frequency but usually the *terminal step* of a review/advisory thread, not a standalone product. *(Harmonized 2026-07-06: an earlier draft said "merely adequately," which contradicted §c-L4's "must be excellent." Both are true at different levels: excellent per instrument, non-central to identity.)*

**What it should refuse to become:** a generation-first drafting tool that treats "draft" as the primary verb; anything that **auto-applies** a disposition, synthesis, or fetch (auto the labor, never the judgment); a system that silently grounds on stale, asymmetric, or unverified inputs. The corpus is unambiguous that Kelly's value-add *is* the judgment — the product's job is to remove the labor around it, not the judgment itself.

**The prioritization principle the failure census proves (promoted from misalignment #5, 2026-07-06):** substantive errors were self-caught wherever a verification step existed — but cross-version corruption, suite non-conformance, and input-parity failures were caught *only* by a second reviewer or by Kelly. **Guardrails covering what the generator cannot see about itself outrank self-verification features.**

**The north-star metric (operator-stated, 2026-07-05, verbatim):** *"This product only provides leverage to the extent that I don't have to go back and copy and paste between models."* Success is measured in **eliminated manual carries per matter** — handoff notes, cross-model pastes, reviewer-feedback round-trips, re-typed correspondence, re-assembled settled terms — trending to zero. Every build decision can be tested against this: does it delete a carry, or add one?

## (d) The misalignment shortlist — where the spec would fail what the corpus proves Kelly needs

Each stated as *spec says X; N threads show Y; the fix is Z-shaped.* Governance flags called out; nothing here silently contradicts a triad-adopted decision.

1. **Generation-forward framing.** The 6-layer architecture and the matter page read drafting-forward. Drafting is the plurality genre but a **minority of the whole** (STRONG on rank-order; exact counts MODERATE, title-inferred — do not cite the raw ratio as precise), and ~40–45% of threads open in review. **Fix:** reorder the matter page so review/verify/disposition are primary verbs and generation is one verb among them. **OPERATOR RULING 2026-07-06: ADOPTED** — binds the C.4–C.6 build (conformance note added to the brief).

2. **No firm-operations surface.** Spec is entirely matter-centric. **~34 threads (15%)** are invoices, staff/HR letters, formatting, training, SOPs, status checks. **Fix:** a lightweight non-matter "operations" lane, or a deliberate decision to leave ops off-platform. Today it runs *through* the matter tool off-label. **OPERATOR RULING 2026-07-06: DEFERRED pending absorption math** — a substantial share of A5 exists *because* of the manual pipeline and evaporates in-platform (engagement letters become matter deliverables; formatting threads are absorbed by house-style generation). Quantify the true residue (likely ~5–7%, invoices/staff/SOPs) before deciding thin-lane vs. off-platform.

3. **Materials assume uploads, not connectors.** Spec grounds matter-state on drag-drop Materials. **ERGUN** reviewed a live 17-file Drive folder; the **NotebookLM** thread is Kelly trying to wire Drive as the shared layer. **Fix:** connector-grounded materials as first-class, with the same provenance/audit as uploads.

4. **Routing under-specified relative to evidence.** Spec labels Dispatch routing MODERATE. **Kelly already task-routes** (Sonnet 4.6 Low for routine correspondence, Opus 4.8 for hard analysis; mid-matter upgrades) across the corpus. **Fix:** encode task-difficulty routing + per-model packaging (named failure modes, row 68). ## (e) What Kelly should stop doing manually — and the handful to keep human

**Absorb into the platform (with the thread-count business case):**
- **Hand-maintaining per-model prompt copies** (rows 10, 57, 68; ~4–9 pure-maintenance threads) → Dispatch per-model packaging. Configure once.
- **The context-overflow handoff ritual** (ERGUN, LOGUE, Kalypsos, and every "thread got too big" moment) → Matter-State Engine. This is the highest-frequency manual tax in the corpus.
- **Assembling the grounded-RAG stack by hand** (NotebookLM thread — per-matter notebooks, statutory silos, disciplined reviewer) → the integrated platform *is* this stack.
- **Cross-checking reviewer citations one at a time** (rows 15, 56, 60) → research-lane verification gate.
- **Re-typing good conversational correspondence into a document** (Ricky email, ERGUN emails) → promote-to-draft, Class 1.
- **Routing tasks to the right model by hand** (Sonnet/Opus selection per task) → Dispatch routing.
- **Re-assembling settled terms across threads at every thread boundary** (Kalypsos, ERGUN) → locked decisions + confirmed facts in matter-state.

**Keep human (deliberately leave un-automated):**
- **The disposition itself** — adopt/modify/reject/hold/pass. Auto the labor of surfacing; never the call.
- **The capacity election** — which hat governs a matter is an attorney judgment.
- **The client-counseling judgment** — when to talk a client down, when to let him hold firm (ERGUN's "his firmness helps you at the table" was Kelly's call to preserve).
- **The final send** — the deliberate-act floor exists for exactly this.

This is the auto-orchestration principle the corpus already proves out: **automate the labor, never the judgment.** The product's identity is the judgment surface; everything else is scaffolding to get Kelly to that surface faster with better-verified inputs.
